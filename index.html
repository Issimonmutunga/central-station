Licences & how this works

verified_user
Nothing is re-hosted. Every download goes straight from the original publisher to your computer. Central Station only builds the links, so the publishers' own licences apply — each dataset page shows its licence and the citation to use.

What's included

  • Direct — open files on public servers. One click (or one script) downloads them.
  • Open portal — free, no account, but the publisher's portal or API is the reliable route. Step-by-step guide included.
  • Free login — needs a free account (Copernicus, NASA Earthdata). Central Station never asks for or stores your credentials; it gives you the steps and generates scripts that use your own keys.

Licence notes

  • WorldClim is free for academic and other non-commercial use; redistribution and commercial use need permission from the authors.
  • Copernicus (ERA5, CMIP6 subset, CORDEX) — CC BY 4.0 for most data; you accept the licence once on the CDS website.
  • NASA, NOAA — U.S. Government open data; cite the dataset.
  • CHELSA, CHIRPS, Berkeley Earth, ISIMIP, TerraClimate — CC BY 4.0 / CC0 — attribution required for CC BY.
  • Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors (tile.openstreetmap.org, light personal use).

Running it

Start the local helper with node server.js and open http://localhost:8787. The helper enables one-click downloads into the downloads folder and streaming map previews. It only talks to the open-data servers listed in the catalog.
